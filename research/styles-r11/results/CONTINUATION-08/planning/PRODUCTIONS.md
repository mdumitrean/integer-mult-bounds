## Exact scalar production

Let i,j,k be distinct registers; a,b are the two original nonzero dyadic coefficients. All equalities below are in **execution order** and hold on arbitrary dirty input values.

Fan-in:

```
i += a*j; i += b*k
    -> j += (b/a)*k; i += a*j; j -= (b/a)*k.
```

The new first gate forms the temporary sum in j; the middle gate adds exactly a*j_old+b*k_old into i; the last gate restores j. Interchanging j,a with k,b gives the second pivot orientation. Original same-target adds commute, but the source order and its literal frames remain the baseline bill.

Fan-out:

```
j += a*i; k += b*i
    -> k -= (b/a)*j; j += a*i; k += (b/a)*j.
```

The first term subtracts the old dirty j from k; the last adds back the updated j, leaving exactly b*i in k. Interchanging the two targets gives the other orientation. These are the inverse-transpose counterparts of the same factorization and form one registered family, not post-result alternatives.

The ratio must itself be an admitted dyadic. Original dyadic a and b do not imply b/a is dyadic (for example a=3/2,b=1). Check the exact reduced denominator is a power of two for each orientation; no floating conversion or coefficient approximation is allowed.
