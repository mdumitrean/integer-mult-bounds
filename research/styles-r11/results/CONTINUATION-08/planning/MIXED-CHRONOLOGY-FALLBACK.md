## A premise change that cannot be reduced to the old selector

Take five disjoint physical copies of the T74 owner geometry. Preserve the complete original 666-call order inside each copy. Their global merge is now a variable, instead of the old fixed order `5*t+k`. This preserves each replica's stage order and each unchanged local program. All unmodified owners remain in their own copy and retain their source call-window relations by projection.

For a moved role at call vertex `x` assigned to an owner interval whose opening and closing call vertices are `L` and `U`, add

`L < x <= U`.

The strict lower boundary is an edge `L -> x`. The upper relation is an edge `x -> U` when the vertices differ, and permits equality only when the exact source owner convention does. Start/end sentinels must follow the existing trusted owner-window semantics. Native calls remain atomic; this is not interleaving scalar instructions inside calls.

Keep all five within-copy call chains and every chosen role-induced edge. A directed cycle is an exact infeasibility witness for that assignment. An acyclic graph admits a canonical topological ordering, from which all owner windows must be re-derived and independently audited. Topological feasibility does not replace same-call physical-family injectivity, actual bin capacities, intact role inventory or compact offsets. Those are checked simultaneously on the final fill.

The width4 groups must be allowed to mix across target F copies. The other width transports likewise need exact indivisible assignments and simultaneous family checks. The existing four independent flows are valid only when the selected destinations really have disjoint physical-family supports. If that premise is relaxed, the model must explicitly couple the affected call/family capacity constraints.

## A useful impossibility lemma and a small positive mechanism fixture

Changing chronology alone cannot rescue the *old homogeneous group rule*. Suppose an original role time `x` is after the closing event `U` of a proposed original destination interval. Mapping all five copies of this one role bijectively to all five copies of that interval induces a permutation `pi` of the copies. The constraints give `x_k <= U_pi(k) < x_pi(k)`. Following any cycle of `pi` is a strict directed cycle. The analogous argument applies when every `x_k` is before the corresponding opening event. This is why the old complete-group-to-one-original-bin rule must not simply be rerun under a different merge.

Mixed source times can escape this particular argument. Consider two copies A and B of the chain

`early < open < close < late`.

Neither early nor late lies in the interval `(open,close]` in the original chain. Yet the merge

`early_B < open_B < early_A < open_A < close_B < late_B < close_A < late_A`

places `early_A` inside B's interval and `late_B` inside A's interval while preserving both original chains. This is a positive fixture for the changed chronological premise, not a full packing candidate or a moment certificate. Its opposite homogeneous-copy permutation supplies a negative cycle fixture. A future complete55-bin fixture would additionally need all width transports, exact occupancy and the charged profile; the small ordering fixture alone is insufficient to approve a scientific family.

## Full invoice and physical scope

The final candidate would keep PR3015's two literal local words, widths, dispatch choice for each original call, 91 private buffers and632-column local interfaces. It would change global order, owner embedding and residual charts. Fresh checks must bind every source word to its admitted native receipt, prove copy composition under atomic restored-helper calls, rebuild the actual call windows, audit all1,358,640 roles and family exclusions, and pay every local and residual child.

The old T74 physical invoice cannot simply be multiplied by five. A scaled namespace, bridge/controller multiplicities, prefix bounds, dedicated child reserve, all finite supplier inequalities and modern outer arithmetic must be checked independently or justified by an explicit conservative transfer. Source receipt hashes do not themselves discharge those changed obligations. The inherited all-size/compiler/theorem conditions remain.

The topological closure here is a legitimate feasibility operation on a named precedence system. It does not give an intrinsic state-independent sandpile Laplacian or cost-decreasing abelian stabilization. Cost comes from the separately certified inventory-neutral pattern circuit and full invoice, not from adding a graph edge or increasing a cycle count.

The useful conclusion is narrow: a mixed-source, changed-chronology construction is a genuine change of premise, whereas merely re-timing the old homogeneous selector has an explicit cycle obstruction. There is not yet enough evidence to promise a realizable or competitively admitted packing family, so this remains an unrun fallback.
