## Constraints for preregistration

Split by mechanism and shared lineage, not arbitrary rows. Base/extra, confirmation seeds, replica copies, overlapping endpoint subsets, re-addressed rewrites and copied source receipts are dependent. Publishedκ, paid profiles, objective deltas, accepted flags, source identity and stage index can leak the label. Define predictors before any feature extraction, preserve source/physical normalization, and report held-out mechanisms separately from a small same-source surrogate fit.

The new [source checkpoint](SOURCE-REVIEW.md) identifies petalas’ active all16V45 subsets/fixed2942owner-bin audit. Do not duplicate that exact experiment. Historical ranking can use existing prices without promoting them to a full-invoice result. Missing costs or labels stay missing. This corpus supports bounded hypothesis ranking; its small number of dependent complete-release episodes does not by itself establish a reliable general predictor of κ improvement.
