# Paired improving releases: structural analysis scope

This is a source-only specification. No feature extraction, control, statistical fit, or new construction was executed by its author. The registered parent runner is responsible for every scientific call through the common pool.

The cohort is `RELEASE-COHORT.json`, SHA256 `3969d32d59be02b88059da8a6e16c21606eb7fadd7c3982c2feda90fe5a4636f`. It contains twelve releases and two paired providers per release. Before/after words and allocation artifacts have exact byte hashes and source credits. The paired parent is the declared structural parent, which can differ from the producer's comparison headline. Both exact reported endpoints and that distinction remain outside the predictors.

## Language and growth measurements

The canonical trusted readers validate the literal BORROW word, its local child profile, and its ordered helper inventory. Rank-free opcode/coefficient and opcode/coefficient/register-role streams include A, cut, B and restore events. The fixed measurements reuse the original M1 implementations of LZ76, LZ78, batched Re-Pair, block entropy at lengths one and six, and repeated 2/3/4-gram counts. Exact scalar operation and role-contact Parikh counts supplement these descriptions. These count scalar instructions and their roles, not paid recursion children.

A separately reported shared binary straight-line grammar is learned jointly from each before/after pair, with at most 201 productions and canonical frequency/tie ordering. Every axiom is expanded back to its exact token stream. This is an actual finite production system, but it is not an inferred iterative or parametric L-system. It does not satisfy or test the old multiword pattern criterion merely by replaying two supplied strings.

Canonical per-register climb strings retain the distinction between ascending climbs and centre descents. Their union independently reproduces the paid local profile. Changed registers, string vocabulary, and order-sensitive entropy are reported as diagnostics. These can restate the profile until an appropriate N2 conditional comparison is performed. No N2 comparison is included here; climb/profile/width-inventory measurements are excluded from the regression features.

The fixed 73 numeric predictors are before/after differences in the rank-free stream measurements, scalar/role counts and graph measurements, plus deterministic scalar edit and frame/endpoint edit counts. No paid histogram, width histogram, moment, exponent, gain, source identity or author is a predictor. Exact rational gain labels are copied separately from the authenticated release cohort.

## Constraint graph and sandpile boundary

The trusted E1 fixed-node constructor builds the actual alias-split BORROW frame-inclusion DAG, retaining parallel register-chain edges, warp endpoint equalities, target/centre contacts and restore contacts. Its entire edge bill plus centre descents must reproduce the canonical local profile. No variable-domain enumeration or solver is used.

For this exact graph, the oriented vertex-edge incidence matrix has integer rank `vertices - connected_components`; every nonzero Smith factor is one by the graphic incidence/forest theorem. Its edge-kernel dimension is `arcs - vertices + connected_components`. The reference undirected operator `B B^T` has nullity equal to the number of connected components. These are genuine exact invariants of the explicitly named constraint graph.

They do not define an intrinsic physical sandpile Laplacian or physical critical group. The established constraint closure is subspace join/intersection propagation with fixed boundary/floor restrictions; projected rank changes depend on orientation and state. Neither this closure nor an arbitrary graph incidence operator supplies a constant-cost physical toppling rule. The two-vertex/two-parallel-arc fixture distinguishes incidence Smith factor one from reduced directed degree determinant two; acyclicity alone does not imply a trivial critical group.

## Packing evidence and missingness

All available authenticated paired FILL/ORDER/DISPATCH/PROVIDERS objects are loaded once in the parent. Where the quartet is present, the worker checks exact role-key uniqueness and completeness, dispatch coverage, ordered word-to-provider helper widths, occupied-cell disjointness, compact occupied prefixes, and same-call exclusion from the identical bin. A reported provider local profile can belong to another stored word version; its equality or mismatch is explicitly diagnostic and never a cost label.

These quartets do not supply a complete trusted PLAN/TEMPLATE binding for every release. Therefore chronological eligibility, capacities and conflicts between different bins of one physical family remain unresolved here. The partial checks are not fresh literal/native admission and must not be presented as complete sandpile or packing analysis.

Moved-role/offset comparisons require equal declared geometry scale, the same ORDER byte hash and the same exact call/helper keys. Different T37/T74/T185 releases are not silently folded or interleaved. Packing-only and precision-only releases remain descriptive cohort entries but are excluded from the word-feature regression. Explicit mixed allocation mechanisms are also censored. An unchanged-word all-zero structural vector means the word mechanism is unobserved, not that a packing improvement had no structure.

## Statistical interpretation

Base and extra are paired views of one release, not independent observations. The learner aggregates them into one release-level observation. Immediate parent blocking and mechanism blocking are reported separately; all twelve releases still have shared ancestry. Releases 2948, 2957, 2966 and the mixed descendant 2979 belong to one shared declared-rewrite family for mechanism holdout.

The regression coverage flag denotes coverage of the declared structural mechanism, not a complete decomposition of the exponent gain. Even a word-changing release can include correlated repacking or invoice changes. Thus the small cohort can support descriptive associations, exploratory gain-size prediction and hypotheses for later preregistration. It cannot establish causal attribution, broad independent-lineage generalization, or a classifier of improvement versus failure: these twelve entries were selected as improving releases. The earlier failed-candidate dataset supplies a different, explicitly local feasibility question rather than interchangeable negative labels.
