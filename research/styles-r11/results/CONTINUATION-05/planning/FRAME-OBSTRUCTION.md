## Orientation363 ←73

Write `M=I+t e_(363,73)`, with the registered nonzero dyadic `t`.
Conjugating B791 introduces the correction

`170 += (t/2)*73`,

before the original363 read. Its frame must contain vector224 because
register73 carries that fixed entrance line. But register170 is target58,
whose fixed final frame is `perp(88)`. Binary dot products give

`224 & 88 =64`, with odd parity.

Thus vector224 is outside that final hyperplane. No monotone target chain
can contain224 at the correction and end inside `perp(88)`. This is an exact
inclusion contradiction, independent of the tariff or any optimizer.

## Orientation73 ←363

Now `M=I+t e_(73,363)`. Conjugating B792 introduces

register194 is target82 with final frame `perp(446)`. Here

`262 & 446 =262 =256+4+2`, again with odd parity.

Consequently this correction also requires a vector excluded by its target's
fixed final hyperplane. No feasible assignment can satisfy that chain.
