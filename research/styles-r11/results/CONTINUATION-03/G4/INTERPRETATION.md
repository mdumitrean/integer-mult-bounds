## A proof question left for a separately authorized future round

A persistent factor-component certificate could strengthen this assignment
observation into a structural neutrality statement. Match graph vertices by
section, original event ID and physical-register/warp-life identity, rather
than mutable numeric node indices. Around each contraction, identify an exact
separator of unchanged boundary vertices that isolates every changed factor.
For every feasible boundary-frame labeling, eliminate the interior variables
and record its optimal conditional cost before and after the rewrite. Equality
of these complete conditional cost tables would prove the component has the
same effect in every surrounding context. Compatible components with such a
certificate could then compose without assuming that disjoint scalar supports
imply independent frame costs.

An integer-surrogate table proves only that surrogate statement. A true-moment
statement would additionally require exact symbolic profile functions or
certified moment comparisons over the relevant endpoint interval, including
all fixed boundary and centre charges. If the separator cannot be isolated or
the tables differ, record that obstruction rather than calling the interaction
modular. No such component certificate was constructed or tested in G4; it is
an explicit remaining proof obligation, not a result inferred from the484
neutral outputs. A generic incidence-matrix Laplacian would add no proof of
this cost equivalence.
